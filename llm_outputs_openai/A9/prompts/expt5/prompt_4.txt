The fix addresses a logic error in the condition that decides when to generate a fresh client random. In the updated logic, the repair reverses the second part of the condition. The rationale behind this correction is to guarantee that every DTLS session that starts a handshake and has not undergone a Hello Verify Request receives an authentically random client random value. The repair thereby removes the flaw where an early DTLS handshake could default to a non-random, fixed value, effectively rectifying the cryptographic misconfiguration.
