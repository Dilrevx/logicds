To fix the vulnerability where zone‐transfer controls were bypassed for writable DLZ zones, the repair always calls the allowzonexfr callback even when a DLZ zone is found in the normal zone table. In the repair, if the zone is identified as coming from a DLZ and is writable (using helper functions “zone_is_dlz()” and “zone_is_writable()”, which you must define or map to the actual checks in your code), we call dns_dlzallowzonexfr to enforce transfer controls. This ensures that even writable DLZ zones are subject to the proper zone‐transfer permissions before proceeding.
