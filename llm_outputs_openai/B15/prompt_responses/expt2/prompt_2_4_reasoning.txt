Detaching the node prevents its reuse, thereby minimizing the risk of memory leaks or subsequent vulnerabilities related to stale data. This loop continues until a match is found or no further candidates exist. This approach eliminates any assumptions about the order or singularity of results in the trust anchor table and guarantees that all possible keys are scrutinized securely.
