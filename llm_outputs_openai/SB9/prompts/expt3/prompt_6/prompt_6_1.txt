This final check prevents a situation where different signature algorithms in the certificate_verify and certificate messages might have been exploited to bypass proper certificate verification.

Overall, the changes ensure that each supported signature algorithm is strictly paired with its corresponding certificate key. By centralizing the validity check into one flag and delaying the error return until all cases have been evaluated, the patch reduces the risk of bypassing the authentication process.
