The root cause of the issue lies in the fact that the provided OSSL_PARAM array is processed after the key and IV have been established when calling EVP_EncryptInit_ex2(), EVP_DecryptInit_ex2(), or EVP_CipherInit_ex2(). This means that any key or IV length parameters specified in the OSSL_PARAM array are not taken into account during the initialization process.

To address this vulnerability, the repair involves processing the key and IV length parameters from the OSSL_PARAM array before the initialization of the cipher. This ensures that the correct key and IV lengths are used during the initialization process, preventing potential truncation or overruns.

By setting the key and IV lengths before the initialization of the cipher, the repair ensures that the correct lengths are used, preventing potential truncation or overruns and maintaining the confidentiality of the cipher modes.
