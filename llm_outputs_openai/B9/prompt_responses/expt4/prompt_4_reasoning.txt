By aggregating the decision of certificate validity into a single "valid" flag and performing the final check only once at the end, the repair ensures that no signature is accepted unless it is appropriately tied to the corresponding certificate. This prevents allowing a TLS client to bypass certificate validation by sending a mismatched signature algorithm compared to what is found in the certificate.
