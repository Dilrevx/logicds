To repair this bug, we must replace the subject argument with the issuer variable. This ensures that the CRL is fetched based on the issuing authority, which is critical for proper revocation checking.

In the provided code, two branches handle different OpenSSL versions. Both branches must be updated to use the issuer when retrieving CRLs. Ensure that the issuer variable is in scope and correctly represents the certificate issuer.
