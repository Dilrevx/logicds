However, if a close-handle callback is already pending, it means that another detach operation is in process. This deferred handling is especially important as it maintains the proper sequence of operations and avoids conflicts that could lead to resource leakage or sockets remaining in an indefinite state (e.g., CLOSE_WAIT).

For threads that are not the designated one, the repair consistently opts to enqueue the detach event rather than calling the detach callback immediately.
