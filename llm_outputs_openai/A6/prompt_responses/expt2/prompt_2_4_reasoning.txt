The reasoning behind the repair centers on the critical importance of nonce uniqueness in the ChaCha20-Poly1305 construction. ChaCha20-Poly1305 is an AEAD (Authenticated Encryption with Associated Data) cipher, which mandates that each encryption operation be performed with a unique nonce (IV). If a nonce is repeated, the security guarantees provided by the cipher are completely compromised, potentially exposing ciphertexts to severe confidentiality and integrity attacks such as key recovery or forging.

This behavior introduces an insidious risk: developers might erroneously believe that modifying the leading bytes of an extended nonce creates a new, unique nonce, not realizing that these extra bytes are ignored. This misunderstanding can inadvertently lead to nonce reuse.

To resolve this vulnerability, the repair replaces the upper bound constant CHACHA_CTR_SIZE with CHACHA20_POLY1305_MAX_IVLEN. This new constant is defined to correctly encompass the maximum accepted nonce length for ChaCha20-Poly1305 operations, in line with the cryptographic protocol’s requirements.
