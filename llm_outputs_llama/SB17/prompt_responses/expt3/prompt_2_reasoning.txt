This oversight allows clients with revoked certificates to connect to the server, posing a security risk.

The reasoning behind this change is rooted in the way CRLs are structured. A CRL typically contains a list of revoked certificates, each identified by its serial number and issuer. The issuer field is crucial in determining the validity of a certificate, as it specifies the entity that issued the certificate. By checking the issuer field, the `tls_verify_crl` function can verify whether the client's certificate has been revoked by its issuer.
