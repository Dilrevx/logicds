2. **Inefficient Loop Structure**: The loops for checking both `subjectAltName` and `subject` fields do not break out early upon finding a match. This inefficiency can lead to unnecessary computations and delays, especially in certificates with many SAN entries.

3. **Misplacement of Mismatch Flag Setting**: The `MBEDTLS_X509_BADCERT_CN_MISMATCH` flag is set inside the loop, which can lead to incorrect flagging if multiple entries are checked and a match is found later in the list.
