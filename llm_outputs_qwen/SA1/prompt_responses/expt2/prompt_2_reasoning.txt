### Reasoning Behind the Repair

The repair addresses a critical vulnerability in the `wpa_supplicant` implementation related to the Protected Extensible Authentication Protocol (PEAP), specifically focusing on the `eap_peap_decrypt` function. The vulnerability arises when `wpa_supplicant` is configured to not verify the network's TLS certificate during Phase 1 authentication, allowing an attacker to send an EAP-TLV Success packet instead of initiating Phase 2 authentication. This can lead to an authentication bypass, enabling an adversary to impersonate enterprise Wi-Fi networks.
