### Contextual Understanding

In TLS 1.3, two primary mechanisms are used for key exchange: Pre-Shared Keys (PSKs) and Elliptic Curve Diffie-Hellman (ECDH) via Key Share Extensions (KSEs). PSKs allow clients and servers to reuse previously established keys, reducing latency and improving performance. On the other hand, ECDH provides forward secrecy, ensuring that even if long-term keys are compromised, past sessions remain secure. Both mechanisms are optional, but they must be handled correctly to maintain the integrity of the session.
