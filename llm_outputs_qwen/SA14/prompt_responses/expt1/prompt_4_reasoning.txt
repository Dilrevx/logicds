### Original Vulnerability

In the context of the SSL/TLS protocol, the "Finished" message is crucial as it indicates that both parties have successfully exchanged their keys and are ready to communicate securely. The client sends a "ClientHello," the server responds with a series of messages including its certificate, and then both parties send "Finished" messages to confirm the successful establishment of the secure connection.

When the client receives the server's "Finished" message, it should proceed to the next step, which involves setting the ChangeCipherSpec (CCS) state to indicate that the subsequent data will be encrypted using the negotiated cipher suite. This is typically followed by sending its own "Finished" message to the server. If these steps are not handled correctly, the client may prematurely declare the handshake complete or fail to encrypt future communications properly, leading to potential security vulnerabilities.
