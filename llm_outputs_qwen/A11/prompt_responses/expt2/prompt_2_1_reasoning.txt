   **Reasoning:** Before checking the revocation status of a certificate, it is crucial to ensure that the Certificate Revocation List (CRL) itself is valid and has not been tampered with. ### 2. **Use of Trusted Time Source**

- **Monitoring and Logging:** Comprehensive logging and monitoring of all certificate validation activities are crucial. Logs should capture details about certificate checks, CRL updates, and any anomalies detected during the validation process. This data is invaluable for detecting and responding to suspicious activities promptly.
