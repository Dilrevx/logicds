The function constructs a chain of certificates starting from the leaf certificate (the certificate being verified) and moving up to a trusted root certificate. ### Identified Vulnerability

The vulnerability lies in the handling of alternate chains. Specifically, when an alternate chain is found, the original chain is discarded without proper validation or comparison. This can lead to security issues because an attacker might craft a malicious chain that appears valid but is not trusted by the intended policy. By simply replacing the original chain with an alternate one without ensuring that the alternate chain meets all the necessary criteria (such as trust policies), the system becomes vulnerable to attacks where an attacker provides a different, potentially malicious, chain that the system accepts.
