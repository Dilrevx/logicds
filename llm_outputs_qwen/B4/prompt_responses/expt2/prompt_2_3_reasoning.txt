1. **Incorrect Size Comparison**: When comparing the sizes of the serial numbers from the certificate and the OCSP response (`rserial.size != cserial.size`), the comparison might fail due to the mismatched sizes. 3. **Security Implications**: An attacker could exploit this vulnerability by crafting an OCSP response with a serial number that includes additional, unintended trailing bytes.
