### Final Cleanup and Resource Management

After exiting the loop, the repair performs a final check to ensure that any remaining `keynode` is detached from the secure roots keytable. This cleanup step is crucial for maintaining the integrity of the keytable and preventing memory leaks. It ensures that no lingering references to key nodes remain, which could otherwise lead to resource exhaustion or security vulnerabilities.

Finally, the repair frees the `keyOut` resource, ensuring that no lingering references to potentially untrusted or invalid keys remain.
