In the context of 5G NAS messages, `registration_accept` and `configuration_update_command` are particularly sensitive because they often carry critical information about the establishment and configuration of a user's session. These messages are typically associated with Security Level (SL) 6, which mandates that they should be protected by integrity checks to ensure their authenticity and prevent tampering.
