### Importance of Initialization Vectors (IVs)

In symmetric key cryptography, particularly in block cipher modes like CBC (Cipher Block Chaining), the Initialization Vector (IV) plays a crucial role in ensuring that identical plaintext blocks encrypt to different ciphertext blocks. For AES-CBC and DES3, the IV must be unpredictable and unique for each encryption operation to prevent patterns from emerging that could be exploited by attackers.
