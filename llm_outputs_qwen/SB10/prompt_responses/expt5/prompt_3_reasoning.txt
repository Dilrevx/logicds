### Vulnerability Explanation

The vulnerability stems from the last step where the IV is zeroed out. In cryptographic operations, especially those involving block ciphers like AES-CBC or DES3, the IV must be both random and unpredictable. The purpose of the IV is to ensure that identical plaintext blocks encrypt to different ciphertext blocks, thereby enhancing security. When the IV is set to zero, this property is lost, making the encryption predictable and susceptible to attacks such as padding oracle attacks or replay attacks.
