For block ciphers operating in modes like CBC (Cipher Block Chaining) or DES3, a unique and unpredictable IV is crucial because the first block of plaintext is XORed with the IV before being encrypted. If the IV is predictable, patterns in the plaintext can be discerned in the ciphertext, undermining the security of the encryption scheme.
