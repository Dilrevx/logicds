In cryptographic contexts, the IV must be unpredictable to prevent attacks such as pattern recognition and chosen-plaintext attacks. A predictable IV, especially one consisting entirely of zeros, can allow attackers to deduce information about the plaintext, undermining the confidentiality of the encrypted data.
