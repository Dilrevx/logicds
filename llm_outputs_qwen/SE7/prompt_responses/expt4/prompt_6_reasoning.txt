### Vulnerability Analysis

In the original code, the handling of NAS messages is divided into two main sections based on the security header type (`sec_hdr_type`). Messages with integrity protection (such as `LIBLTE_MME_SECURITY_HDR_TYPE_INTEGRITY` and `LIBLTE_MME_SECURITY_HDR_TYPE_INTEGRITY_AND_CIPHERED`) are checked for integrity before processing. Messages marked as plaintext (`LIBLTE_MME_SECURITY_HDR_TYPE_PLAIN_NAS`) are processed without any integrity checks. This distinction is crucial because certain sensitive messages, such as those mentioned in the vulnerability description, should never be transmitted in plaintext due to their potential impact on the network's security.

### Repair Strategy

The core of the repair involves removing the cases corresponding to Detach Request, EMM Information, and EMM Status from the `switch` block. This change ensures that these messages are only processed if they are accompanied by appropriate security measures, such as integrity protection.
