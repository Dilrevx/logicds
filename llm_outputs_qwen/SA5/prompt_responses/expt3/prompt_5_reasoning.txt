### Understanding the Context

In the context of digital security, OCSP is a protocol used to check the status of a digital certificate without needing to download and parse a large Certificate Revocation List (CRL). When a client receives a digital certificate from a server, it can use OCSP to verify that the certificate has not been revoked by the Certificate Authority (CA). This verification is crucial for maintaining the integrity and security of the communication channel.

The `OCSP_NOCHECKS` flag is a configuration option that, when set, instructs the verification process to skip certain checks, potentially reducing the overhead but also compromising security. In the absence of this flag, thorough checks must be performed to ensure the certificate's validity.
