### Understanding the Vulnerability

In 5G networks, NAS messages are used for communication between the User Equipment (UE) and the Access and Mobility Management Function (AMF). These messages can be sent in various forms, including plain text or encrypted and integrity-protected formats, depending on the stage of the NAS security context establishment process.

When a UE initially establishes a connection with the AMF, it may send NAS messages in plain text (`plain_5gs_nas_message`). However, once NAS security has been activated, these plain text messages should no longer be accepted as they pose a security risk due to lack of encryption and integrity protection.
