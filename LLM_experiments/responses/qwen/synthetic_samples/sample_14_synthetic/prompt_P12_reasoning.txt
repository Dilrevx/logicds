2. **Lack of Logging/Debugging Information**: Without logging the error, diagnosing issues becomes challenging, especially in production environments where detailed logs are crucial for troubleshooting.
3. **Security Risks**: In some cases, an attacker could exploit the lack of proper error handling to infer information about the system's internal state or to cause a denial-of-service attack by inducing errors that terminate the handshake process.
