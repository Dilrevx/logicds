### Understanding the Vulnerability

In a standard TLS handshake, the ChangeCipherSpec (CCS) message plays a crucial role in signaling a transition from unencrypted to encrypted communication. According to the TLS specification, the CCS message must be received by the recipient immediately before the Finished message, which confirms the successful establishment of the secure connection. If the CCS message is received at an incorrect time, it can disrupt the handshake process and lead to security vulnerabilities.
