### Vulnerability Explanation

The vulnerability arises from the fact that the IV is not being used correctly after its generation. Specifically, the line `ssl.lastIv = args.iv;` assigns the reference of `args.iv` to `ssl.lastIv`. Subsequently, the loop that zeros out `args.iv` also zeros out `ssl.lastIv` because they refer to the same array. As a result, `ssl.lastIv` ends up being an array filled with zeros, which is not a random IV and thus violates the security requirements of AES-CBC and DES3 encryption modes.
