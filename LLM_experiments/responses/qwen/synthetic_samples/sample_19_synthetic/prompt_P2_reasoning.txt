A key aspect of these certificates is the digital signature, which is used to verify the authenticity of the certificate itself. The signature is created using a specific cryptographic algorithm, such as RSA-SHA256 or ECDSA-SHA384.

The vulnerability in the original code lies in the lack of validation for the signature algorithm used in the certificate.
