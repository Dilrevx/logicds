The vulnerability stems from the fact that the `allowzonexfr` method is not being called for writable DLZ zones. In DNS, zone transfers are critical operations that can expose the entire contents of a zone, including sensitive records like IP addresses, hostnames, and mail server information. Without proper control over who can perform these transfers, unauthorized parties might gain access to this information, leading to security risks.
