- **Sanitization:** Ensure that the JIDs do not contain any malicious characters or sequences that could lead to injection attacks.
- **Whitelist:** Optionally, compare the JIDs against a whitelist of known and trusted JIDs. This adds an additional layer of security by explicitly allowing only certain JIDs.
