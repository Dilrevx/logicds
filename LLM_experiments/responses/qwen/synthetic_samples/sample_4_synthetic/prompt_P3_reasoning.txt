### Understanding the Vulnerability

**RC4-MD5 Ciphersuite**: This is a combination of the RC4 stream cipher and the MD5 hash function, used for encryption and integrity checks in TLS/SSL protocols. The security of this ciphersuite has been deprecated due to vulnerabilities in both RC4 and MD5, but the specific issue here relates to improper handling of the MAC key.

**Incorrect Use of AAD as MAC Key**: In authenticated encryption with associated data (AEAD) modes, AAD is data that is included in the MAC computation but not encrypted. It is meant to ensure the integrity of additional data that must remain unencrypted. However, using AAD as the MAC key is a severe design flaw. The MAC key should be derived securely from the shared secret established during the TLS handshake, ensuring it is unpredictable to any external observer.
