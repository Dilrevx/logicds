The primary risk associated with this vulnerability is unauthorized access to account management functionalities. An attacker gaining access to these features could lead to significant security breaches, including:

- **Data Leakage**: Unauthorized access to user data can result in sensitive information being stolen or exposed.
- **Privilege Escalation**: Attackers might exploit the lack of authorization checks to elevate their privileges, gaining access to even more sensitive areas of the system.
- **Account Compromise**: By changing passwords or altering user roles, attackers could lock legitimate users out of their accounts or grant themselves administrative privileges.
