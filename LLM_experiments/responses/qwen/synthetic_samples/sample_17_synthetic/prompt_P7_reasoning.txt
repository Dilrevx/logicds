This step is crucial because it prevents the client from engaging with potentially harmful or unexpected authentication methods that could be used by an attacker to exploit vulnerabilities.

If there are acceptable methods available, the client selects one and proceeds with the corresponding authentication process. This selection can be based on various criteria, such as the order of preference in the client's acceptable methods list or other security considerations.
