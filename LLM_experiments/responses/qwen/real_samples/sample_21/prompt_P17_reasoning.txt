In TLS 1.3, selecting the appropriate cryptographic group (also known as curve) is crucial for key exchange operations. The group determines the elliptic curve used for ECDHE key exchange, which is fundamental to the security of the connection. Without setting the correct group, the key exchange may fail or use an insecure curve.

**Repair:**

### Key Share Configuration
Key shares are essential in TLS 1.3 for establishing a shared secret between the client and server. The key share is generated based on the chosen group and is used during the key exchange process. Properly configuring the key share is necessary to ensure that the key exchange can proceed without errors.

**Repair:**
