This prevents other users, including malicious guests, from accessing or modifying the contents of the directory. By restricting access in this way, the risk of unauthorized access to sensitive resources is significantly reduced.

   - **Repair Context:** By setting the directory permissions to `0700`, the repair ensures that only the process itself can interact with the directory, thereby preventing malicious actors from exploiting it to gain access to host devices or other sensitive resources.
