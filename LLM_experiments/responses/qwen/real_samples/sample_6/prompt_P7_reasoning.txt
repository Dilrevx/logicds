### Understanding ChaCha20-Poly1305

ChaCha20-Poly1305 is a widely used authenticated encryption scheme that combines the ChaCha20 stream cipher with the Poly1305 message authentication code. This combination provides both confidentiality and integrity for encrypted data. One of the key components of this algorithm is the nonce, a number used once in a cryptographic communication. The nonce plays a crucial role in ensuring that the same key can be reused securely across multiple encryption operations without compromising security.

### The Importance of Nonce Length

On the other hand, a nonce longer than 12 bytes could introduce additional complexity and potential errors in handling the nonce without providing any additional security benefits.
