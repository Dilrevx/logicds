### Vulnerability Explanation

In cryptographic systems, the choice of signature algorithm is critical for ensuring security. Weak or deprecated signature algorithms can make the system vulnerable to attacks such as forgery or man-in-the-middle attacks. Therefore, it is essential to verify that the signature algorithm used by a certificate is strong and supported by the system.
