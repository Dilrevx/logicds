### Role of CRLs in TLS Handshake

Certificate Revocation Lists (CRLs) play a vital role in maintaining the integrity of secure communications over the internet. A CRL is a list of certificates that have been revoked by their issuing Certificate Authority (CA). During the TLS handshake, the server (or client, depending on the configuration) verifies the client's certificate to ensure it has not been revoked. If the certificate appears in the relevant CRL, the connection should be terminated to prevent potential man-in-the-middle attacks or unauthorized access.

### Why the Issuer Parameter is Necessary

For CRL validation, the correct parameter to use is the `issuer`. This is because CRLs are issued by the CA that signed the certificate being verified.
