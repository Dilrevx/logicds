In the context of CRL verification, it is crucial to check both the issuer and the subject fields of the CRL entries. The issuer field identifies the CA that issued the certificate, and the subject field identifies the certificate itself. By checking only the subject field, the system might miss valid revocations because the CRL entry does not match the subject field as expected due to the incorrect logic. This can result in allowing connections from clients whose certificates have been revoked, thus compromising the security of the TLS/SSL connection.
