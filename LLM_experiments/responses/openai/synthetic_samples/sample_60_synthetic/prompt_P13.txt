Explanation:
The repair fixes a potential vulnerability that could arise when using getBytes() without specifying a charset. Without an explicit charset (for example, UTF-8), the conversion is platform‐dependent—which may allow an attacker, who has access to the Prometheus web.yml configuration file, to influence or bypass the authentication logic. By explicitly using UTF-8 encoding, the behavior becomes predictable and secure regardless of the underlying system’s default charset.
