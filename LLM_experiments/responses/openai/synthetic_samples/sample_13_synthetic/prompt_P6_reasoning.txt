The repair removes the conditional check that prevented the zone transfer control from being applied consistently. In the buggy version, dlzAllowZonexfr was only being called if client.view.dlz_searched was not empty. This meant that for some writable DLZ zones, the allowzonexfr method was never invoked, making the zone transfer controls ineffective.

In the repair, dlzAllowZonexfr is always called when the initial result is not successful, ensuring that the authorization logic for zone transfers is applied regardless of the state of client.view.dlz_searched. This adjustment fixes the vulnerability by guaranteeing that the DLZ zones, even those that are writable, go through the necessary validation via allowzonexfr, thereby reducing the risk of unauthorized zone transfers.
