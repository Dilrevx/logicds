Forcing the FIN Handshake Completion – The vulnerability occurs because if the TCP stream is specially crafted, the connection can end up stuck in CLOSE_WAIT when one end has already sent a FIN (indicating closure) but the local side hasn’t fully closed the connection. By calling isc_socket_shutdown() with the ISC_SOCK_SHUT_RDWR option, the socket is told to stop both receiving and sending data. This forces the underlying TCP stack to send the FIN (if it hasn't already) and, importantly, to respond properly to any incoming FIN. Consequently, it ensures that the FIN/ACK exchange between the two endpoints completes without indefinitely waiting with the connection in CLOSE_WAIT.

2. Prevention of Resource Leakage – Without explicitly shutting down the socket, the connection might remain in an undesired state, causing lingering resources that could potentially be exploited or simply lead to resource exhaustion. The explicit shutdown helps release the resource more deterministically.
