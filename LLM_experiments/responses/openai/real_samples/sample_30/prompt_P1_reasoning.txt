   After retrieving the CRLs, two separate stacks are maintained (crls_subject and crls_issuer). The repair then creates a new stack (crls) and iterates over both stacks, merging their entries into this new unified list. This merge process guarantees that all relevant CRL entries are included in the verification process.

   The repair carefully frees the temporary stacks containing the subject and issuer CRLs after merging. This helps prevent memory leaks while ensuring that the newly allocated combined stack (if successfully created) is used in further processing.

   If allocating the new combined CRL stack fails, both temporary stacks are immediately freed. This defensive programming practice avoids potential crashes or undefined behaviors when the system is under low-memory conditions.
